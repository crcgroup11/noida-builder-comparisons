## Why Does the Registration Year Matter?

ATS Destinaire was registered in 2019, so it is a later stage project that is closer to handover. CRC Maesta was registered in 2024 and is earlier in construction. A buyer who needs a home in the near term will look at the stage first. A buyer who is comfortable waiting can compare the later project on design and layout.

## What Should You Ask About a Completion Date That Has Been Extended?

When a declared completion date has passed and an extension is cited, ask the developer for the current date registered with UP RERA, the progress of each tower and any written handover schedule. The same questions apply to every project, including ours.

## Which Buyer Is Each Project Suited To?

- ATS Destinaire suits a buyer who wants a project closer to handover and who can confirm the revised date in writing.
- CRC Maesta suits a buyer who prefers a compact community of 5 towers and who plans for completion in May 2029.
