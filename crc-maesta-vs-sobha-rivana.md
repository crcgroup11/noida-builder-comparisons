## What Does the Completion Date Gap Mean for a Buyer?

The declared completion date for CRC Maesta is about 20 months earlier than the date declared for Sobha Rivana. A buyer who wants to move in sooner, or who is paying rent in the meantime, can weigh that gap against every other factor in the decision.

## How Different Are the Project Sizes?

CRC Maesta covers 3.64 acres with 5 towers. Sobha Rivana covers 11.76 acres, and Sobha states that nearly 80 percent of the site is kept as open area. A smaller project means fewer neighbours and a smaller community. A larger project can carry more shared facilities. Choose the scale that fits how you want to live.

## Which Buyer Is Each Project Suited To?

- CRC Maesta suits a buyer who prefers a compact community and an earlier registered completion date.
- Sobha Rivana suits a buyer who prefers a large site with a large open area and who is comfortable with a later completion date.

## How Can You Check These Facts Yourself?

Search the registration numbers above on the UP RERA website, up-rera.in, and compare the declared completion date, area and promoter name. Ask each developer for the current price sheet in writing.
