# CRC Group vs Gaurs Group in Greater Noida West: Maesta and Aspire Centurian Park Compared

**Disclosure:** This page is published by CRC Group. Figures for Gaur Aspire Centurian Park come from its UP RERA registration certificate and Gaurs' own project updates. Last updated 2 November 2026.

Gaurs Group is a long established developer in Greater Noida West, known for its township Gaur City. CRC Group is a smaller developer. Their current projects in a comparable range are CRC Maesta and Gaur Aspire Centurian Park, so this page compares those two.

## What Is the Difference in Completion Dates?

CRC Maesta declares completion about sixteen months before Gaur Aspire Centurian Park. Both dates are filed with UP RERA.

## How Different Are the Project Sizes?

Gaur Aspire Centurian Park is a much larger project, at about 12 acres, and it sits within a Gaurs area of Techzone 4. CRC Maesta is a compact community of 5 towers on 3.64 acres. Decide which scale suits your family.

## How Should You Compare Delivery Records?

Ask both developers for the list of projects they have completed, the possession year of each and the occupancy certificate. Visit those projects and speak to residents. A delivery record is best judged by visiting, not by reading a web page.
